Hi, I'm Verrier Wilson!

I am a (born 8 June 1955),computer scientist best known as the inventor of the World Wide Web, HTML, the URL system, and HTTP. I am a professorial research fellow at the University of Oxford and a professor emeritus at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT).I proposed an information management system on 12 March 1989 and implemented the first successful communication between a Hypertext Transfer Protocol (HTTP) client and server via the Internet in mid-November.I devised and implemented the first Web browser and Web server and helped foster the Web's subsequent development.
